# Anchor papers from initial scouting (2026-07-25)

Working notes, not the corpus. IDs verified live at scouting time. One line each on why
they matter to the paper.

## The auditability subthread (core of RQ2 framing)

- **2607.09195** — Toward Auditable AI Scientists: Hypothesis Evolution Protocol (HEP).
  Makes hypothesis→test→belief cycles explicit, auditable operations. Direct evidence the
  field recognizes the auditability gap.
- **2607.05682** — FirstResearch: auditable question formation, Research Question
  Certificates. Same thread, at the ideation stage; nb. evaluated with LLM judges only —
  itself an instance of the gap.
- **2606.31273** — The Calibration Turn in AI-Assisted Research: evidence-licensed claims
  framework; "no claim without license". Our conceptual neighbor; we provide the empirical
  map it lacks.
- **2607.17100** — Auto Research for Materials: auditable AI-scientist workflows with
  held-out transfer. Exemplar of STRONG verification (held-out survival of selected
  changes) — the positive pole of RQ2's matrix.
- **2607.02329** — Grounded autonomous research: corpus→manuscript pipeline in
  computational physics (11,083-paper corpus, 47 sessions, bounded human intervention).
  Closest methodological cousin to our own reflexive setup; different object domain.

## Integrity literature (motivation + Related Work)

- **2606.02184** — The Ghost Couple: correlated fake author names across model families;
  1,655 ghost-authored Zenodo records with real DOIs, deliberate backdating. THE foil for
  our disclosed-AI-authorship approach. Note for our Zenodo plan: our record must be the
  anti-pattern — full disclosure, real human, verifiable process.
- **2607.10712** — Distributed Denial of Science: indirect data poisoning of autonomous
  research agents, 49.56% attack success, 6% detection; provenance auditing reduces to 0.
- **2605.03723** — Segmenting human-LLM co-authored text via change point detection
  (minimax-optimal); the detection side of co-authorship.
- **2603.01718** — PLOS ONE bibliometrics post-2022: manuscript length up, NNES team
  composition changes. Bibliometric-method neighbor.
